Under the hood

How the engine thinks

A ply is one player's move. "Depth 6" means the engine looks three moves ahead for itself and three replies for you.

Minimax

The engine builds a tree of future positions. At its own turn it picks the child with the highest score, at your turn it assumes you pick the lowest. Scores bubble up to the root. This implementation uses the negamax form: one function, with the sign flipped at every ply.

score(pos) = max over moves of −score(child)

Alpha-beta pruning

Alpha is the best score the engine can already guarantee. Beta is the best score the opponent can already guarantee. When a branch scores at least beta, the opponent would never allow it, so the remaining siblings are skipped. The answer is identical to minimax, found by looking at far fewer positions.

if alpha ≥ beta: cut off, skip the rest

Move ordering

Pruning works best when the best move is searched first. The engine tries immediate wins first, then forced blocks, then moves that caused cutoffs earlier (the history heuristic), with a bias toward the centre columns. Perfect ordering can shrink the tree from about bd toward bd/2.

Iterative deepening

Search to depth 1, then 2, then 3, and so on until time runs out. Early passes are cheap. Each pass finds a best move that is searched first on the next pass, which improves pruning. It also means the engine always has an answer ready, so it can obey a time limit.

Evaluation functions

When the search stops before the game ends, a leaf position needs a score.

  • Basic: each square has a weight equal to how many four-in-a-row lines pass through it. Centre squares are worth the most. Score = your weights minus the opponent's.
  • Advanced: looks at all 69 possible lines of four. Open threes score +50 (−55 for the opponent's, so it defends first), open pairs +6, and centre-column discs +6 each.

Reading "Why this move?"

  • Alternatives: alpha-beta only proves that other moves are no better than the best one, so they show as "≤ score".
  • Expected line: the principal variation, the sequence both sides play if both play best.
  • Position table: the evaluation features measured after the chosen move.
  • Win in N: a score near ±100000 means the search proved a forced result.
